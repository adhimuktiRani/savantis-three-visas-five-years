Renewals

Applying is a promise. Renewing is proof.

On day one, you're judged on your plans. At renewal, you're judged on what actually happened: the salary you were paid, the company you kept active, the days you spent here, the deposit you kept in place. And by the rules in force on the day you renew, not the day you were approved.

Within five years, most pathways will ask you to prove you still qualify. And every pathway expects you to keep qualifying the whole time.
Vik mentioned that in Malaysia renewal is harder than the first application. The official rules explain why: renewals are judged on evidence and on the rules in force at the time (EP salary bands changed for renewals from 1 Jun 2026; Labuan work permits need proof the company is active; MM2H extensions need the deposit still under lien). This is also one of the most-asked questions in the Facebook groups. The evidence a renewal asks for is produced by exactly the services Savantis runs after approval: accounting, tax, company upkeep.

For the Savantis team

The process behind "Three Visas, Five Years"

1 · Strategic logic

The problem

  • Prospects arrive visa-first: "I need MM2H".
  • Every guide compares entry requirements, so Savantis looks like one more option on price.
  • Lead research: the top worry is "who can I trust?"

The insight

  • Savantis' advantage starts after the visa: company, bank, tax, family, renewals.
  • Savantis is a boutique lead adviser, not an agent. The real alternative is managing several providers yourself.

The decisions

  • Compare futures, not entries.
  • Answer first, comparison second (changed after testing v1).
  • Built on official rules, so it works without assumed fees.

7 · What could go wrong

  • Rules change (EP bands changed 1 Jun 2026, for renewals too). "Last checked" date, monthly review, one owner.
  • Read as eligibility advice. "Indicative" labels; an adviser confirms on the call.
  • Year-five content is judgement. Written with Savantis, framed as "things to plan for".
  • Too many watch-outs. Capped at 3, in a fixed priority order.
  • A question Savantis can't answer well. Reviewed with Vik first; a weak answer is a service gap worth knowing.
